## Problem

Designing VR interactions that feel natural requires careful tuning of locomotion, hand presence and spatial feedback.

## Role

I prototyped the interaction system and room-scale locomotion, iterating on comfort and presence.

## Stack

A Unity project using the XR Interaction Toolkit and C# for grab, teleport and physics-based interactions.

## Result

A comfortable room-scale prototype demonstrating grab interactions and teleport locomotion.
